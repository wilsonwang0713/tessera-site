Support

Tessera is a business-card scanner that keeps your contacts, the context of how you met them, and your network graph on your phone.

Questions, bug reports, feature requests: email wilson07131@gmail.com. Include your iOS version and, if it helps, a screenshot.

Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and, if you enable it, in your own iCloud. We do not run an account system and we cannot see your cards.

What does cloud recognition send?

Only the card image, only to be read. It is not stored on our servers. You can turn cloud recognition off at any time and use on-device recognition instead.

How do I export or delete everything?

Export to vCard, CSV, JSON, or a full backup from inside the app, free of charge. Deleting the app removes all local data; iCloud data can be removed from iOS Settings → iCloud.
